## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an authoritative, reliable, and modern civic news and commercial directory platform. It balances institutional journalistic credibility with high-converting, accessible touchpoints for local businesses and citizens.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Citizens & Commuters:** Seeking immediate, accurate, day-to-day community pulse (weather, utility notices, regional developments, emergency phone directories) with immediate trust.
- **Local Business Owners & Sponsors:** Seeking verified local reach, commercial growth, and high-visibility placements through frictionless onboarding and prominent calls-to-action.
- **Emotional Response:** Trustworthy, modern, utilitarian, yet energetic and commercially vibrant.

### Design Movement
The visual language fuses **Modern Corporate/Civic** clarity with **High-Utility Digital Editorial**. It eschews sterile minimalism in favor of structured visual segmentation: clean micro-surfaces, subtle layered borders, purposeful status tags, and luminous accent triggers for high-intent conversion moments.

## Colors

The color architecture differentiates content integrity from commercial utility:

- **Primary (`#0F2942` - Deep Navy):** Anchors headers, primary navigation, institutional titles, and authority markers. Provides editorial weight and high contrast.
- **Secondary (`#0066FF` - Electric Blue):** Drives direct action, key interactive affordances, links, and commercial CTA banners (`Anuncie aqui`, `Cadastre seu Negócio`).
- **Tertiary (`#00D2FF` - Cyan Surge):** Accent dynamic state indicator; used in weather widgets, progress cues, active border highlights, and technology/data points.
- **Neutral (`#475569` - Slate):** Regulates secondary editorial text, metadata, timestamps, and subtle framing lines.
- **Named Accents:**
  - `accent-amber` (`#F59E0B`): Dedicated to commercial highlights (`Destaque Comercial`), sponsored badges, and critical notices.
  - `badge-emergency` (`#EF4444`) & `badge-success` (`#10B981`): Functional signifiers for civic emergency lines (190, 192, 193) and verified business statuses.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural and display clarity with **Inter** for dense, comfortable editorial reading.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to command attention and maintain structural density in fast-scrolling news environments.
- **Body & Digest:** Set in Inter with generous line-heights (1.55 to 1.6) to guarantee legibility across varied informational lengths (news briefs, legal notices, directory entries).
- **Labels & Badges:** Set in uppercase or capitalized Plus Jakarta Sans with distinct tracking (`0.04em` - `0.06em`) to ensure instant differentiation between `NOTÍCIA`, `INFORMATIVO`, `PUBLICIDADE`, and `SERVIÇO PÚBLICO`.

## Layout & Spacing

The portal adheres to a 12-column responsive layout system maxing out at `1200px` for optimal informational scanning and ad placement ergonomics.

### Layout Rhythm
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` outer page margins. News items, weather widgets, and utility telephone cards use standardized multi-column spans (e.g., 6 columns for half-width emergency cards, 4 columns for sponsor tiles).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters. Content flows into dual-column cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `0.75rem` gutters and `1rem` safe margins. All horizontal informational clusters collapse into vertical stacks with unified card paddings.

### Component Spacing
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-spacing between tags and secondary metadata icons.
- `space-sm` (8px): Spacing inside chips, pill badges, and compact button insets.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard internal card padding and stack gaps.
- `space-lg` (24px): Inter-section header margins and prominent lead card paddings.
- `space-xl` (40px): Distinct section separators across editorial domains.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system uses low-elevation surfaces supported by crisp, low-contrast structural outlines rather than heavy theatrical drop shadows, maintaining institutional trust.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` base surface creates warm contrast behind cards without clinical glare.
- **Level 1 (Standard Card & Feed Tile):** `#FFFFFF` background bound by a 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0` and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Sponsored & Commercial Highlight):** Dedicated commercial tier (`Destaque Comercial`) featuring a calibrated amber border stroke (`1.5px solid #F59E0B`) with an ambient amber-tinted shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Lead Generation Hero & Modals):** Applied to commercial acquisition banners (`Anuncie seu Comércio`) using deep navy/blue gradient backdrops combined with high-depth elevation: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

A medium roundedness level (`roundedness: 2` / 8px base) balances civic formality with approachable modern digital design.

- **Base Cards & Modules:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) radius provides crisp, contained modularity without looking toy-like or severe.
- **Lead Containers & Hero CTAs:** `16px` (`1rem`) radius elevates primary marketing callouts and weather forecast blocks.
- **Badges, Chips & Action Buttons:** Full pill geometry (`9999px`) for categorization chips (`NOTÍCIA`, `SERVIÇO PÚBLICO`) and quick contact actions to provide immediate interactive affordance.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Lead CTAs
- **Primary Action (`Anuncie aqui` / `Cadastre seu Negócio`):** Background `#0066FF`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded pill (`9999px`) or `8px`, font `label-lg`. On hover, shifts to `#0F2942` with subtle transform scale.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent fill, `1.5px` border in `#0066FF`, text `#0066FF`. Hover state applies `rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.06)` tint.
- **Conversion Hero Block:** Full-width container with gradient background (`135deg, #0F2942 0%, #1D4ED8 100%`), white text, and a vibrant `#00D2FF` or `#F59E0B` CTA action button.

### 2. Category Chips & Visual Badges
- **News Chip:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#0F2942`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
- **Informativo / Aviso:** Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1D4ED8`, border `1px solid #BFDBFE`.
- **Publicidade / Destaque:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
- **Serviço Público:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#047857`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`.

### 3. News Feed Cards
- Composed on Level 1 surface with `space-md` internal padding.
- Structure: Micro-badge (`NOTÍCIA`) + timestamp and source attribution row, followed by an authoritative semi-bold title (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), brief excerpt, and explicit source outbound link in `#0066FF`.

### 4. Commercial & Sponsor Cards (`Destaque Comercial`)
- Highlighted frame with amber top-border or full outline (`#F59E0B`).
- Includes commercial badge, business name (`headline-sm`), category tag, telephone/WhatsApp contact links with high tap-target sizes, operating hours, and verified website anchors.

### 5. Emergency & Public Utility Directory Cards
- Compact 2-column or 4-column cards featuring oversized telephone dialing numbers (e.g., `193`, `192`, `199`) in `#0F2942` display sizing alongside immediate 24-hour service status indicators.

### 6. Weather Widget Header Bar
- High-density card presenting real-time temperature, condition badge, multi-day forecasting segments with subtle dividers, and regional indicator (`São Carlos/SP`).

### 7. Form & Lead Inputs
- Standard inputs built with `44px` minimum tap height, `8px` corner radius, `#FFFFFF` fill, `#CBD5E1` border, focusing to `#0066FF` with `3px` focus ring `rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.2)`.
